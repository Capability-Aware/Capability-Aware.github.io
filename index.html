DEMO PROJECT

Nerfies: Deformable Neural Radiance Fields

Keunhong Park, Utkarsh Sinha, Jonathan T. Barron, Sofien Bouaziz, Dan B Goldman, Steven M. Seitz, Ricardo Martin-Brualla

ICCV 2021

Turning casually captured videos into portraits that can be viewed from new angles. Nerfies models a moving subject using a deformation field and a neural radiance field.

DEMO PROJECT

HyperNeRF: A Higher-Dimensional Representation for Topologically Varying Neural Radiance Fields

Keunhong Park, Utkarsh Sinha, Peter Hedman, Jonathan T. Barron, Sofien Bouaziz, Dan B Goldman, Ricardo Martin-Brualla, Steven M. Seitz

ACM Transactions on Graphics · 2021

Modeling dynamic scenes whose shapes and connections change over time. HyperNeRF adds higher-dimensional coordinates to represent changes that are difficult to capture with a continuous deformation alone.
